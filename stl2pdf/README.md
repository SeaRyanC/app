## STL2PDF – STL Cross-Section Viewer

STL2PDF lets you load any `.stl` file and interactively explore cross-sections through it, then print those sections to an accurately dimensioned PDF. The app runs entirely in your browser — no file is ever uploaded to a server.

Open an STL file by dragging it onto the page, clicking to browse, or pasting. The model is shown in three orthographic views — Front (XZ), Side (YZ), and Top (XY) — with Z pointing up. Click anywhere in a Front or Side view to place a cutting plane, which instantly renders the 2D cross-section in the Section Output pane. The indicator line can be dragged up or down to move the plane, and right-clicking it cycles the plane orientation through horizontal (Z), vertical-X, and vertical-Y. Every pane supports zoom (scroll wheel) and pan (right-click drag).

In the Section Output pane, the cross-section is drawn engineering-drawing style: interior material shaded light gray, empty space white, and a thick black outline. When you're happy with the slice, click **Print PDF** to download an 8.5 × 11" PDF with the cross-section at true 1 : 1 millimetre scale (scaled down only if the geometry is larger than one page). A scale bar, plane label, and section dimensions are included on every sheet.
