## Bus Planner

Factorio Bus Planner is a visual, drag-and-drop planner for Space Age main buses. Search the complete bundled Factorio 2.1.17 item and fluid catalog, drop materials into the field to create lanes, and drop materials below the field to add stations to a single master list. Each station can be flipped to tap from the left or right side of the bus with one click, and can be nudged up or down the list with move buttons (in addition to drag-and-drop reordering). Lanes stop at their last tap instead of extending beyond the planned factory, and an inline remove control makes accidental lanes easy to dismantle.

Clicking a station opens a popup showing its standard recipe's inputs for reference, alongside checkboxes for every lane currently in the plan; the lanes matching that recipe are pre-checked when the station is created, and can be freely toggled afterward -- there's no separate recipe-selection step. Hovering a station traces each input from its originating output tap to the hovered station in a distinct resource color, while hovering a supply lane highlights its full route and every station that pulls from it. Lanes connect to the producing station nearest the selected flow edge, with a solid spine in the normal direction and a dashed counterflow segment when needed. The field toggle switches between the default bottom-to-top flow and top-to-bottom flow, and applies equally to normal and collapsed views. Multiple taps on one station are spread into their own rows to minimize crossings, and any tap that must cross a lane it isn't connecting to is drawn with a small schematic "hop" so crossings never read as connections. For dense plans, collapse view starts from the normal lane order and assigns a one-sided lane to the nearest compatible slice on its station side, processing from the outer edges inward; its chevrons follow each segment's physical compacted direction. Collapse view is intentionally read-only so the compact rendering stays unambiguous.

The catalog and recipe list are generated from Factorio's merged base, Space Age, and installed expansion prototype data, with the corresponding local icon assets bundled into the app. Plans autosave locally and can be shared through the URL. Lanes and stations can be reordered without throughput calculations, making this a quick layout tool for exploring factory organization.
